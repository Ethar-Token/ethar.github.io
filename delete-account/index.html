Delete Your ETHAR Account

If you would like to permanently delete your ETHAR account and request deletion of your associated personal data, you can submit a deletion request to our support team.

How to Request Account Deletion

Send an email to:

support@ethartoken.com

Please send the request from the email address associated with your ETHAR account and include your ETHAR account email address in the message.

Request Account Deletion

What Will Be Deleted

Once your deletion request has been verified and processed, your ETHAR account and associated personal information will be deleted where applicable.

This may include:

  • Your account profile
  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your date of birth
  • Your country information
  • Other personal information associated with your account

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, financial, or other legitimate purposes. Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Important

Account deletion is permanent. Once your account and associated data have been deleted, the account may not be recoverable.

Need Help?

If you have questions about account deletion or your personal data, contact us at support@ethartoken.com .
